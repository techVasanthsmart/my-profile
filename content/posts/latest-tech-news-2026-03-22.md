Here are the top tech headlines for 2026-03-22, summarized for busy readers.

1) Microsoft launches MAI-Image-2 (AI image model, gen-2)
Microsoft announced MAI-Image-2, a second-generation AI image model that improves photorealism and reliability when generating images with text. It’s rolling out across Copilot and Bing Image Creator, promising better image quality for creators and integrations inside Microsoft products.

2) Meta expands AI moderation and reduces reliance on contractors
Meta said it will widen deployment of its AI-assisted moderation systems for Facebook and Instagram, intending to reduce reliance on third-party human contractors. The company will still keep human reviewers for nuanced cases, but routine enforcement tasks will increasingly be automated.

3) Samsung confirms Galaxy S26 will support AirDrop-like sharing
Samsung executives announced Galaxy S26 will support AirDrop functionality, expanding cross-device sharing on Android. This follows Google’s moves to broaden Android’s proximity-sharing features and could improve cross-platform workflows.

Quick take
These stories show large tech companies doubling down on AI capabilities — from creative tools (Microsoft) to content moderation (Meta) — while hardware makers (Samsung) focus on smoother device interoperability. Expect continued friction around AI’s role in replacing human work, alongside product improvements that push convenience forward.
